## Research Context

I list this project in my public CV and link it to the paper "Influence of image artifacts on image-based computer simulations of the cardiac electrophysiology."

## Approach

The source abstract describes two model series: one based on a clinical MR image where slice translations are applied to induce and correct slice misalignment, and one based on computer-simulated MR images created with and without slice misalignments.

## Evaluation Context

The work assesses how slice-aligned and slice-misaligned models respond to different interval stimuli in the context of tachycardia-risk simulation. The public description reports that misalignment can affect simulation outcomes, with effects depending on infarct geometry and conducting-channel preservation.
