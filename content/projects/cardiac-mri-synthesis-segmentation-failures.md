## Approach

The public abstract describes a model for segmenting cardiac structures in a multi-centre, multi-disease, and multi-view scenario. The pipeline combines heart-region detection, image synthesis for augmentation, and late-fusion segmentation.

## Evaluation Context

The source description frames the work around reducing segmentation failures on difficult outlier cases and improving consistency in downstream derived metrics.
