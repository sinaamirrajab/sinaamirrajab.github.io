## Research Context

I describe this PhD thesis work in my public CV as simulation and synthesis for cardiac magnetic resonance image analysis, conducted at Eindhoven University of Technology within the OpenGTN project.

## Approach

The thesis develops and investigates approaches for generating realistic CMR images with corresponding ground-truth labels for supervised cardiac tissue segmentation.

My public CV describes two connected directions:

- physics-driven image simulation grounded in MR image-formation physics and the Bloch equations;
- data-driven image synthesis using conditional deep generative models.

## Thesis Context

My public CV identifies the thesis as "Realistic Cardiovascular Magnetic Resonance Image Simulation and Synthesis for Medical Image Analysis" and lists Prof. Dr. Ir. Marcel Breeuwer as supervisor.
