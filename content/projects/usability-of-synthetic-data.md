## Research Context

I list this project in my public CV and link it to "On the usability of synthetic data for improving the robustness of deep learning-based segmentation of cardiac magnetic resonance images."

## Approach

The source abstract describes short-axis CMR image synthesis using a segmentation-informed conditional GAN trained on paired real images and segmentation maps for the heart and surrounding tissue.

## Evaluation Context

The work evaluates synthetic data in replacement and augmentation settings across datasets from multiple sites and scanners. The public description frames synthetic data as a way to study and mitigate domain shift in medical-image segmentation research.
