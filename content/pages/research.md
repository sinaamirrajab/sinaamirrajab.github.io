My research spans cardiac MRI and cancer CT/MR imaging, with a focus on foundation models, LLMs, retrieval-augmented generation, multimodal vision-language models, and latent diffusion models for image synthesis and clinical decision-support research.

The thread connecting these projects is simple to say and hard to do: make medical AI more data-efficient, more interpretable in context, and more useful for the kinds of questions radiology and clinical research actually ask.

I describe these systems cautiously on purpose 🧪. A research prototype is not the same thing as a clinically deployed tool, and I do not claim clinical validation unless reviewed source material supports it.
